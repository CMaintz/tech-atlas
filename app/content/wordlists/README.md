# Plain-language wordlists

The "plain everyday language" base for the Closed Vocabulary lint (E1).

- `en.txt`, `da.txt` — the 30,000 most frequent words per language, alphabetised.
- Source: Hermit Dave, _FrequencyWords_ (2018, derived from OpenSubtitles),
  https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords — licensed
  [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Do not hand-edit these. Technical words that are allowed but will never be Terms go
in `../allowed-words.<lang>.txt` instead.
